# Vendor Management Policy

| Version | 1.0 |
|---|---|
| Owner | CEO |
| Approved by | Pending |
| Next review | 12 months after approval |

## Purpose
Make sure the companies we rely on protect our
data as well as we do.

## Scope
Any vendor that stores, touches, or supports
Monvive Cloud data or systems.

## Risk tiers
- High: handles customer data
  (Supabase, Stripe, Google Workspace)
- Medium: supports systems but no customer data
  (Netlify, GitHub, Slack)
- Low: everything else

## Policy
1. Every vendor is risk-tiered before we sign. (C15)
2. High-tier vendors must provide a SOC 2 Type II
   report or answer our security questionnaire. (C15)
3. High-tier vendors are reviewed every year. (C15)
4. Contracts must include confidentiality and breach
   notification terms.
5. We keep a list of all vendors and their tiers.
6. Key vendors need a backup plan in case they
   go down. (C20)
7. When a vendor relationship ends, our data is
   returned or deleted.

## Roles
- CEO: owns this policy and approves new vendors
- CTO: reviews vendor security documents

## Exceptions
Must be approved in writing by the CEO.
