# Acceptable Use Policy

| Version | 1.0 |
|---|---|
| Owner | Ops Manager |
| Approved by | Pending |
| Next review | 12 months after approval |

## Purpose
Set clear rules for how employees use company
devices, accounts, and data.

## Scope
All employees and contractors, on any device used
for Monvive Cloud work.

## Policy
1. Never share your password or MFA codes.
2. Lock your screen when you step away.
3. Customer data stays in approved systems only.
   No personal email, USB drives, or personal apps.
4. Don't install unapproved software on company
   laptops.
5. Field tech phones must have a screen lock and
   remote wipe turned on. (C13)
6. Report a lost or stolen device within 1 hour.
7. Report suspicious emails. Don't click, forward
   them to the CTO. (C09)
8. Company systems may be monitored for security.
9. Every employee signs this policy at hire and
   every year. (C17)

## Enforcement
Violations may lead to discipline up to termination.
