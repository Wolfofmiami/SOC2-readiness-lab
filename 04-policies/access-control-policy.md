# Access Control Policy

| Version | 1.0 |
|---|---|
| Owner | CTO |
| Approved by | Pending |
| Next review | 12 months after approval |

## Purpose
Make sure only the right people can get into
Monvive Cloud systems, and only as much as they need.

## Scope
All employees and contractors. Systems: booking app,
Supabase, Google Workspace, GitHub, Netlify, Slack.

## Policy
1. Least privilege: people get only the access their
   job needs. (C05)
2. New access must be approved by a manager before
   it is granted. (C03)
3. MFA must be turned on for every in-scope system. (C01)
4. Admin access is limited to the CTO and named
   developers. (C05)
5. All access is removed within 24 hours of an
   employee's last day. (C02)
6. The CTO reviews all user access every quarter
   and documents the results. (C04)
7. No shared accounts unless the CTO approves and
   documents it.
8. Passwords must be 12+ characters and stored in
   the company password manager.

## Roles
- CTO: owns this policy and runs access reviews
- Ops Manager: requests and removes access
- Employees: protect their logins and report lost
  devices right away

## Exceptions
Must be approved in writing by the CTO, documented,
and re-checked every 90 days.

## Enforcement
Violations may lead to loss of access and discipline
up to termination.
