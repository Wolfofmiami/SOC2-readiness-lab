## Purpose
Make sure Monvive Cloud catches, handles, and learns
from security incidents fast.

## Scope
All employees, contractors, and in-scope systems.

## What counts as an incident
Anything that threatens the privacy, accuracy, or
availability of customer data or company systems.
Examples: stolen login, lost phone, data leak,
ransomware.

## Severity
- High: customer data exposed or systems down.
  Respond within 1 hour.
- Medium: possible exposure, contained.
  Respond within 24 hours.
- Low: no data at risk. Respond within 3 days.

## Policy
1. Anyone who suspects an incident must report it
   to the CTO immediately.
2. Every incident follows these phases: detect,
   contain, fix, recover, lessons learned. (C11)
3. Every incident is logged with a timeline and
   actions taken.
4. Customers are notified as required by law and
   contracts. Legal counsel is consulted first.
5. The team practices this plan with a tabletop
   exercise every year. (C11)

## Roles
- CEO: incident lead and final decisions
- CTO: technical lead (containment and recovery)
- Ops Manager: communication with customers and staff

## Enforcement
Failing to report a known incident may lead to
discipline up to termination.
